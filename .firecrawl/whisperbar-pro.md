## Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You only need internet once to download the Whisper model. After that, WhisperBar works completely offline.

Which model should I choose?

For German and other European languages we recommend "Small". For Japanese and Chinese, use "Medium" or larger. For maximum accuracy, choose "Large v3".

Does WhisperBar work with every app?

Yes. WhisperBar inserts text via the Accessibility API and works in any app with a text field — Notes, Mail, Safari, Slack, VS Code and more.

What is the Enhance feature?

Enhance is an optional second step that runs a small on-device language model (Gemma 4) after transcription to tidy up grammar, punctuation and self-corrections. It runs 100% locally, you can turn it on or off at any time, and it never sends your text anywhere.

Are there subscriptions or hidden costs?

No. WhisperBar costs a one-time €9.99. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no hidden fees. All future updates are included.

Which macOS version is required?

WhisperBar requires macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later.

What devices are supported?

WhisperBar runs on all M-series Macs — M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, and future Apple Silicon chips. Intel Macs are not supported. We recommend at least 8 GB of RAM, as WhisperBar uses the larger Whisper Large V3 Turbo model for better accuracy.
